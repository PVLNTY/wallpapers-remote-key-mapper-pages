Set it up

1. Make a bot

Open @BotFather in Telegram and create a bot just for your TV. Keep the token private.

2. Connect your TV

Enter the bot token in Wallpapers. Send the one-time code from the app to your bot in a private chat.

3. Send photos

Send photos to the bot. Then choose how often the TV changes its wallpaper.

Your data stays on your TV

Wallpapers encrypts the bot token on your TV. Photos stay in the app's private storage. Do not add your bot token to this repository or share it online.

The app needs internet to sync photos. Android may delay background sync while the TV is asleep.
